    How the schedule works

    • Very difficult: the word comes back tomorrow.
    • Some effort: the word stays at its level, so a daily word stays daily.
    • Easily: the word moves up a level and the gap grows: 2 days, 4 days, a week, 2 weeks, then a monthly check once mastered.
    • The first time a new word comes up sets its starting point. Read easily, it skips ahead to every 4 days.
    • Each list opens and closes with a well-known word, with easier words spread between the tricky ones.
    • Stars: every word scored earns stars (3 easily, 2 with effort, 1 very difficult), so trying always counts.
    • Streak: any day with at least one scored word keeps the streak going.
    • Star total: stars add up over time, and every 500 stars sets off a big fireworks show.
    • Streak bonuses: every 7 days in a row earns 10 bonus stars and a celebration. Every 30 days in a row earns 50 more and a bigger show.
    • More than one round a day: after finishing a list, start another round. It uses words not done yet that day, because scoring the same word twice in one day would throw off its schedule. Use the quick re-run to repeat words without scoring.

    Backups and other phones

    Words and progress are saved only on this device. Save a backup now and then. To move to another phone or share with another grown-up, send the backup file and restore it there. Restoring replaces what is on that device, so practice on one device at a time.
