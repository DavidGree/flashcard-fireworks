# Flashcard Fireworks

**Open the app: https://davidgree.github.io/flashcard-fireworks/**

Daily flashcard practice for young readers. Each day's list mixes tricky words with ones the reader already knows, and the gaps between reviews grow as words stick. Every scored word earns stars and plays the same cheerful ding, so effort counts. Finishing a list sets off fireworks, and the app keeps a daily streak. Every 7 days in a row earns 10 bonus stars, every 30 days earns 50 more, and every 500 stars sets off a big fireworks show.

It's free, with no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your child's words, scores and name stay on your own device and are never uploaded. It works offline once it has been opened.

## What it runs on

- iPhone and iPad (use Safari)
- Android phones and tablets (use Chrome)
- Chromebooks, and Windows or Mac computers (Chrome or Edge)

## Getting started

1. Open the link on the device your child will practice on.
2. Add it to the home screen so it opens like an app:
   - **iPhone or iPad (Safari):** tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**.
   - **Android (Chrome):** tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
   - **Chromebook or computer (Chrome or Edge):** click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or look for **Install** in the browser menu.
3. Open it from the new icon. Type your child's first name if you like, then tap **Add the class word lists** and tick the lists you want, or type your own words.
4. Go to **Today**, tap **Make today's list**, and score each word as your child reads it: **Easily**, **Some effort** or **Very difficult**. The app decides which words come back and when.

## Good to know

- Each device keeps its own words and progress, so practice on one device.
- On iPhone and iPad, add the app to the home screen before adding any words. Words added in Safari don't carry over to the home-screen icon.
- Private or incognito windows don't save anything, and clearing the browser's data erases the words.
- Save a backup now and then (**Progress**, then **Save a backup file**). To move to another device, send the backup file there and use **Restore from a backup**.
